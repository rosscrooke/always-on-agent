# In-Flight Entertainment Ad Creative Policy

All creatives served on the in-flight entertainment (IFE) network must comply with the rules below. The compliance routine scans creatives against this policy and flags violations.

## Alcohol
- Alcohol creatives are permitted **only** with visible responsibility messaging ("Please drink responsibly" or equivalent) **and** age-gating metadata (21+).
- Alcohol creatives must **not** be placed adjacent to kids/family content zones.
- **Violation:** alcohol creative with no responsibility messaging, no age-gating, or placed in a family/kids zone.

## Misleading claims
- No absolute or guaranteed-outcome claims ("guaranteed", "100% effective", "cure").
- **Violation:** any unsubstantiated absolute claim.

## Restricted categories
- No gambling, tobacco/vaping, political, or prescription-pharma creatives on the IFE network.
- **Violation:** any creative in a restricted category.

## Landing / click-through
- Click-throughs must resolve to a working, brand-safe destination over HTTPS.
- **Violation:** broken, non-HTTPS, or off-brand landing page.

## Accessibility
- Video creatives must include captions.
- **Violation:** video creative with no caption track.
